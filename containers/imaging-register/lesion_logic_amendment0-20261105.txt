Reason: while writing the harness I applied the code's own internal floor to the scenarios and found my expectations for the smallest
spheres did not account for it. findings_runner.py sets MIN_LESION_ML = 0.2 (a connected component under 0.2 mL is dropped as speckle
and never reported, "about 7 mm"). A sphere of diameter d has volume pi d^3 / 6: 6 mm is 0.113 mL (dropped), 8 mm is 0.268 mL (kept
on a fine grid; on thick slices the voxel count can fall under the floor).

      graded only where it is reported.
