Change decided: a mesh over the face budget is made by marching cubes at a coarser step (2 to 8 voxels, grid padded by the step, mask smoothed with a
Gaussian of 0.3 times the step), taking the first step whose mesh is closed, manifold and within the budget. If no step does, the full-resolution mesh
is kept and the record says so. The registered OP13 field "decimated" now means "reduced".

Added criteria
OP13b closed manifold. For synthetic organs that include a thin-walled hollow shell and a shape with a thin bridge, at face budgets 40000 and 5000 and
      with no budget, every mesh the builder writes has 0 boundary edges and 0 edges shared by more than 2 faces, the mesh volume is within 5 percent
      of the voxel volume, and the face count is within the budget when the record says it is.
OP14  frame caution. A deformable-frame mesh record carries a caution naming the scans in which the organ is cut by the scan range, when the organ is cut
      in either scan of the pair, and carries none otherwise.
OP15  physiology note. Change rows carry volume_varies_with: filling (stomach, duodenum, small_bowel, colon, urinary_bladder, gallbladder, esophagus),
      inspiration (lung organs), cardiac and contrast phase (heart, aorta, inferior_vena_cava, portal_vein_and_splenic_vein); no other organ carries one.
      General physiology only, no threshold and no clinical call.
