SCENARIOS (1.0 mm isotropic unless stated; classes 23 and 26)
  V1 volume: spheres of 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 30 mm at spacings 1.0x1.0x1.0, 0.7x0.7x1.25, 0.8x0.8x3.0; reported volume_ml against pi d^3 / 6
  V2 long axis: reported long_axis_mm against d (same sweep)
  V3 linking, shift sweep: one stable lesion of 8, 12, 20 mm in two timepoints, the second shifted 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 14 mm along x:
     is it one track with two members?
  V4 split: one 20 mm lesion at timepoint 0; at timepoint 1 two 12 mm spheres with centres 17 mm apart: the topology reported for each track
  V5 merge: the reverse of V4: topology reported
  V6 category rules: recist_category for the cases of test_findings_recist.py, with a nadir equal to the baseline unless stated

PASS CRITERIA (fixed now)
  T1 volume: for d of 8 mm or more, |reported - true| / true <= 10% at spacings 1.0 and 0.7; at 0.8x0.8x3.0 reported, no limit
  T2 long axis: as LK1 of the findings test: |measured - d| <= max(1.5 mm, 10% of d), d of 8 mm or more, every spacing
  T3 linking: a stable lesion is ONE track with two members for every shift at which the two spheres still overlap (shift < d)
  T4 split: the docstring says the code tags split and merge; the criterion is that V4 reports topology "split" for the lesion and
     that V5 reports "merge"
  T5 category: the RECIST 1.1 category for every V6 case (new measurable lesion before CR; PD needs 20% and 5 mm over the nadir)
