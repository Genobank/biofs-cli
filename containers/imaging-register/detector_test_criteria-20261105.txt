Detector known-answer test of the findings verb (findings_runner.py in imaging-findings:v27-20261105, VISTA-3D detector, LesionLocator tracker if its checkpoint is present)
Pre-registration, written before the first run. Engineer: Daniel Uribe. Date 2026-11-05.
Companion of lesion_logic_criteria-20261105.txt (logic with injected masks, sha256 0d986a78...): this test uses the REAL detector.

DESIGN
One real chest CT (the Jun 17 scan of job register-ef1676c3, 1.0 mm isotropic, derived from the owner's own series; it stays on the GPU VM disk and is never copied to the laptop).
Spheres of known diameter and density are blended into the lung parenchyma (known_answer.insert_sphere, 0.8 mm soft edge), at seeded positions at least 12 mm from the lung edge, in plain parenchyma, at least 35 mm apart.
Each scan gets independent Gaussian noise of 10 HU (baseline seed A, follow-up seed B), as a stand-in for two acquisitions. No blur, no deformation, no rigid offset: the registration is the easy case.
The executor runs unchanged (real main(), real VISTA-3D, real tracker). Only storage is replaced: a `gcloud` shim on PATH maps gs://bucket/path to a local folder, so nothing is read from or written to the vault.
Series are written as DICOM by synthetic_dicom.py (synthetic identifiers only), baseline study date 2026-01-01, follow-up 2026-04-01.

PAIRS (baseline -> follow-up)
  D0 no change              baseline and follow-up are the same CT with different noise. Nothing inserted.
  D1 new nodules            same baseline as D0. The follow-up gets 8 new nodules: solid (40 HU) of 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 30 mm and one ground-glass (-500 HU) of 16 mm.
  D2 growth                 baseline: solid 12 mm (a) and 16 mm (b). Follow-up: a 18 mm (+50%, +6 mm), b 16 mm.
  D3 shrink and disappearance  baseline: solid 20 mm (a), 16 mm (b), 14 mm (c). Follow-up: a 12 mm, b 10 mm, c absent.

WHAT IS READ OUT (per pair, from the executor's findings.json, its per-instance masks and the truth file)
  detection:  for each inserted nodule, the best-overlapping instance in the scan where it exists: IoU with the true sphere, measured long axis, volume ratio
  findings:   status and category the executor reports, against the truth
  tracker:    which tracker ran (LesionLocator or overlap fallback), the classes VISTA-3D accepted and dropped

PASS CRITERIA (fixed now)
  DT1 sensitivity: reported by diameter and density (no threshold: this is a measurement of the detector)
  DT2 no change: D0 has no finding with status new or enlarged
  DT3 measurement: for every nodule with IoU >= 0.2, |measured long axis - true diameter| <= max(2.0 mm, 15% of the true diameter)
  DT4 status: for every nodule detected in the scan(s) where it exists: D1 new; D2 a enlarged, b stable; D3 a shrunk, b shrunk, c resolved
  DT5 category consistency: the category reported for each pair equals RECIST 1.1 (as stated in lesion_logic_criteria-20261105.txt) applied to the executor's own findings
  DT6 clinical consequence (reported, not graded): the category the truth would give (D1 PD, D2 PD, D3 PR, D0 none) against the one reported, and every inserted nodule the detector missed

PREDICTIONS WRITTEN BEFORE THE RUN (scored after it)
  E1 VISTA-3D does not detect most of the inserted solid nodules; none of the 6 mm and 8 mm ones; at most half of the solid ones of 12 mm and above (low confidence)
  E2 the 16 mm ground-glass nodule is not detected
  E3 D0 gives no new or enlarged finding (moderate confidence: the same anatomy with other noise)
  E4 the LesionLocator checkpoint is present and the tracker that runs is LesionLocator (low confidence; if absent the overlap fallback runs)
  E5 for the nodules that are detected the long axis is above the true diameter (the bias of the logic test)
  E6 at least one pair gives a category that differs from the truth because a nodule was missed

LIMITS (stated before the run)
  Inserted spheres are not tumours: they are smooth, uniform and have no vessels or spiculation. A detector that misses them says little about real lesions; one that finds them says little as well. The test measures the executor end to end on a case with a known answer, not clinical sensitivity.
  One CT, one patient's anatomy, one set of seeds. Registration is trivial (same grid). The Jun 17 scan may contain real structures VISTA-3D calls tumours; they appear in both scans and are reported.
  The executor image and its model weights are those of the VM on the day of the run; their identifiers are recorded in the report.
