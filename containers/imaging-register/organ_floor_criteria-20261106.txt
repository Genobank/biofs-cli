Organ volume floors: pass criteria and predictions, written before organ_floor.py existed and before any floor run. Date of writing: 2026-11-06.
Module: organ_floor.py. Tests: test_organ_floor.py. Package side: organ_package.py (amendment 2 below, same file of criteria).

Question: how much does the volume of an organ, as TotalSegmentator draws it, change between two scans when nothing about the organ has changed?
A measured answer lets the organ package say "this change is larger than the noise of the measurement", and nothing more. It does not say a change
is clinically relevant. A floor is a lower bound of the real variation between two visits: it holds no filling of hollow organs, no change of posture
or of arms, no change of contrast phase beyond what the sources contain.

Three sources, each "no true change" by construction:
  S1 perturbation of the operator's own scans. Each native scan (3 scans of the chest job register-ef1676c3, 3 of the abdomen job register-e05d1252, 1 mm
     isotropic CT as the jobs staged it) gets 6 emulated variants, then every variant and the unperturbed CT are segmented again by the production path
     (register.segment, TotalSegmentator total). Variants: V1 white Gaussian noise 10 HU (seed 1); V2 noise 10 HU (seed 2); V3 noise 20 HU (seed 3);
     V4 Gaussian blur 0.6 mm; V5 a shift of 0.5 mm along each axis (linear resampling); V6 blur 0.6 mm plus noise 10 HU (seed 4). V0 is the unperturbed
     CT segmented again. Value per organ, scan and variant: |volume(variant) / volume(job's own native labels) - 1| in percent. An emulation, not a
     reconstruction: it shows the sensitivity of the segmentation to noise, blur and sub-voxel position of this scan.
  S2 real same-day repeats: RIDER Lung CT, the 8 pairs already segmented for the lung-mass calibration (rider_repeat, 16 scans, same scanner, same kernel,
     minutes apart: repositioning, breath and noise realisation are real). Value: |volume(B) / volume(A) - 1| in percent, scans in acquisition order.
     Chest field of view only.
  S3 real reconstruction change: RIDER Lung CT, 6 patients, the lung-kernel and the standard-kernel reconstruction of the same raw data at 1.25 mm and
     at 2.5 mm, each segmented (24 segmentations, 12 pairs). Value: |volume(standard) / volume(lung) - 1| in percent.
Organs: the 29 of organ_package.DEFAULT_ORGANS (composites lung_left, lung_right, spine as in the package). A value is dropped when the organ touches
the edge of the scan (organ_package.scan_edge) in either scan of the comparison, because its volume is then a lower bound.

Floor per organ and source: n, median, p95 and max of the absolute relative difference in percent, and the p95 in ml; only when n >= 8, otherwise null.
Floor per organ for the package: the largest p95 percent over the sources with n >= 8. The floors file lists for each organ which sources it
came from, and states what the floor excludes (filling, posture, arms, contrast phase; for the organs with no same-day repeat, every real source).

Criteria on the code, known-answer, all must pass (test_organ_floor.py)
FL1 perturbation. Noise: the standard deviation of (variant - original) is within 3 percent of the requested value on a 60 cubed volume; the same seed gives
    byte-identical output and another seed does not. Blur: the effective sigma of an impulse is within 5 percent of the requested value. Shift: a smooth
    test image shifted by 0.5 voxel equals the analytic shifted image within 2 percent of its amplitude, away from the border. Zero noise, zero blur and zero
    shift return the original exactly. Origin, spacing, direction, size and pixel type of the output equal the input.
FL2 volumes. The volume of an organ from a label map equals voxel count times voxel volume exactly and the cut flag equals organ_package.scan_edge.
FL3 pooling. For a made list of differences p95, median, max and n equal numpy to 1e-9; a value is dropped when the organ is cut in either scan; n < 8 gives
    null; the package floor is the largest p95 over the sources with n >= 8; the floors file carries sources, limits and the exclusion text; the output is
    deterministic (same input, same bytes apart from the time).
FL4 application in the package (organ_package.py, amendment 2). With a floors file, each change row of an organ with a floor carries floor_pct and
    exceeds_measured_floor: true when |delta_pct| > floor_pct, false when not, null when the row is not assessable (the reason is kept). An organ without
    a floor carries floor null and exceeds_measured_floor null. The words clinically relevant appear nowhere in the package. Without a floors file nothing
    changes: repeatability_floor stays null and no row carries exceeds_measured_floor. The organ's change record names the floor sources and the
    exclusions. verify rejects a package whose recorded floor differs from the floors file hash it names.

Criteria on the real data (organ_floor_real_checks.py reads the outputs; nothing here is tuned after the run)
RF1 completeness. The run produces 21 segmentations per job in S1 (3 scans x 7 variants incl. V0), 16 in S2 (read from disk, no new segmentation) and 24 in S3.
    Every segmentation that fails is listed; none is replaced.
RF2 coverage. For each source the number of organs with n >= 8 is reported with the list of organs that have none; the abdomen organs (liver, spleen,
    kidneys, pancreas, gallbladder, stomach, duodenum, small_bowel, colon, urinary_bladder, prostate, adrenals) must have a floor from S1.
RF3 determinism of the segmenter. V0 equals the job's own native labels in volume to 0.5 percent for every organ of 20 ml or more in all 6 scans; if not, the
    result is reported as segmenter non-determinism and the floors already contain it.
RF4 the floors file is built, hashed and read back; the package is rebuilt for both jobs with the floors and verify reports OK.

Predictions, written before any run
P-F1 V0 is identical to the job's own labels (volumes equal) for every organ in all 6 scans.
P-F2 S1 p95 of lung_left, lung_right, liver, spleen, kidney_left, kidney_right and heart is at most 2 percent.
P-F3 S1 p95 of adrenal_gland_left, adrenal_gland_right, gallbladder, duodenum and pancreas is at least 5 percent.
P-F4 S1 p95 of colon and stomach is at most 6 percent (a floor that cannot see filling).
P-F5 For at least 60 percent of the organs that have both, the S3 p95 is larger than the S1 p95 (a kernel moves boundaries more than noise does).
P-F6 S2 p95 of lung_left and lung_right is between 3 and 15 percent (breath).
P-F7 On the abdomen job the change of liver at jan9 against mar3 (+6.3 percent), of spleen at oct21 (+7.2 percent) and of pancreas at jan9 and oct21 exceed
     their floors; the changes of kidney_left and kidney_right at oct21 (-0.1 and -1.2 percent) do not.

Not in this version, stated now: abdominal same-day repeat scans (none available), inhale against exhale, the effect of contrast phase, a floor for
filling of hollow organs, floors for each scanner or protocol, a floor for the shape of an organ, any threshold for clinical relevance.
